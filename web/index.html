Data integrity

Designed to fail honestly.

01

Verified prices only

Local London fares come from TfL Journey Planner; flight fares come from live flight providers. FareSphere does not manufacture a replacement fare.

02

Live rail context

National Rail departures are displayed separately from fare search so timetable data is never mistaken for ticket pricing.

03

Source provenance

Every result records its provider and whether its price is verified or sample data.
